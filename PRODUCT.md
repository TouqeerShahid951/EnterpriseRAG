# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Enterprise administrators, knowledge managers, reviewers, and authorized knowledge workers operating document intake, retrieval, governance, and evaluation workflows. They need clear system state, predictable controls, and traceable evidence while working with sensitive information.

## Product Purpose

Prudentia AI is a local enterprise RAG workspace for controlled document intake, grounded retrieval, evidence inspection, OCR review, auditability, evaluation, and artifact generation. Success means users can safely prepare and query governed knowledge while understanding what the system is doing and retaining control over optional, resource-intensive processing.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and trustworthy. The interface should feel capable and restrained, with direct language and dependable interaction patterns.

## Anti-references

Avoid consumer-style playfulness, decorative AI spectacle, unexplained automation, noisy dashboards, and unfamiliar controls that make routine enterprise work harder to understand.

## Design Principles

- Keep users in control of optional or costly processing.
- Make system state and consequences visible before actions are taken.
- Preserve evidence, provenance, permissions, and auditability throughout every workflow.
- Prefer familiar, consistent controls over novelty.
- Support dense operational work without sacrificing clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic labels, sufficient color contrast, reduced-motion preferences, and status communication that does not rely on color alone.
